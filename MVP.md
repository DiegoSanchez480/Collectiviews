# Project Proposal: Movie Review App

# The MVP

## Point of View

- Who: Casual moviegoers
- Who NOT: Hardcore movie fans, Professional Critics

## Entities

- What: User, review, movie, watchedMovie
- What NOT: Category, movie contents

## Actions

- When: Login, view, review
- When NOT: recommend, direct message, watch

## Links

- Where: User and review, user and watchedMovie, movie and review, movie and watchedMovie
- Where NOT: user and user, review and watchedMovie

## Actions

- Why: View movie, make new review
- Why NOT: watch movie, message users, recommend movies
